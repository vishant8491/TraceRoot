INCIDENT DESCRIPTION (OBSERVED EVENT):
During final batch release testing of Candesartan Cilexetil API lot CC-2026-B089, QC Laboratory identified an unexpected, unspecified impurity peak at Relative Retention Time (RRT) 1.42 via reverse-phase HPLC (Method QC-HPLC-CC-04, Agilent 1290 Infinity II system). The impurity measured at 0.28% w/w, exceeding the ICH Q3A / Finished API Specification limit of NMT 0.10% w/w (Individual Unknown Impurity). 

PRIMARY TEST DATA:
- Assay: 99.4% (Specification: 98.5% - 101.5%) - PASS
- Water Content (KF): 0.18% (Specification: <= 0.30%) - PASS
- Total Impurities: 0.72% (Specification: <= 1.00%) - PASS
- Unknown Impurity RRT 1.42: 0.28% (Specification: <= 0.10%) - OUT OF SPECIFICATION (OOS-2026-0142)

IMMEDIATE CONTAINMENT ACTIONS TAKEN:
1. QC Laboratory logged laboratory OOS investigation OOS-2026-0142 under SOP-QC-088.
2. Lot CC-2026-B089 (145.2 kg finished API in double polyethylene lined fiber drums) was quarantined in Drum Storage Suite D-12 with physical quarantine warning tags.
3. ERP system status for lot CC-2026-B089 placed on "Quality Hold - Inactive" preventing warehouse transfer or packaging.
4. Intermediate batch CC-INT-2026-B090 currently in Reactor R-202 was paused at the trityl-deprotection stage pending root cause clarification.

INITIAL INVESTIGATION FINDINGS:
Laboratory Phase 1 Investigation ruled out analytical error:
- System suitability passed (column efficiency > 5000 theoretical plates, tailing factor 1.05).
- Re-injection of standard and sample preparations from original vials confirmed 0.28% peak.
- Fresh sample preparation by a second analyst on a separate HPLC instrument (Waters Alliance e2695) reproduced the identical 0.28% peak at RRT 1.42.
Conclusion: Genuine process-related batch deviation, not a laboratory artifact.

SUSPECTED PROCESS AREA & TIMELINE:
- Synthesis Step 4: Trityl group deprotection of Candesartan trityl ester intermediate using aqueous hydrochloric acid in methanol / toluene biphasic mixture.
- Reaction conducted in 3000L glass-lined reactor R-201 on 2026-10-16.
- Historian trend review indicates that at 14:22 UTC on 2026-10-16, the reactor jacket temperature control valve (TCV-201) experienced a hunting cycle. Reactor internal temperature peaked at 58.4°C for 42 minutes (Operating parameter window: 40°C - 45°C).
- Prolonged heating under acidic conditions is known to promote over-hydrolysis of the ester linkage, forming the des-ethyl carboxylic acid degradant typically eluting at RRT 1.42.

OPERATIONAL CONTEXT & EQUIPMENT LOGS:
- Maintenance log PM-R201-Q3 showed actuator diaphragm for TCV-201 was flagged for replacement during the previous turnaround but was deferred due to lack of spare part stock.
- Shift handover log between Shift A and Shift B did not document the 42-minute temperature excursion as an alarm because high-temp audible alarm was silenced during shift changeover.
